SOLMARA BAY

Jay Kessler came back to Solmara Bay broke, with a busted delivery van and a debt to the wrong people. One harbour job at a time, he is going to buy his way out — or get caught trying. Drive the coast, steal what you need, lose the cops, finish the jobs.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D — walk / drive  ·  Shift — run
Mouse drag — look around  ·  Space — jump / handbrake
E — enter or leave the nearest car (stealing in front of cops raises your wanted level)
F — punch  ·  Click — shoot your pistol  ·  R — reload
M — mission list  ·  P / Esc — pause & save  ·  U — mute sound
Crimes raise your wanted stars. Hide from the police and the heat fades. At 3 stars the Solmara Bay Police set up roadblocks. Finish missions to earn cash and unlock the next one.
